Petrol cut by Rs8 a litre, diesel by Rs7 — new rates from today

The federal government has cut petrol by Rs8 a litre to Rs338 and high-speed diesel by Rs7 to Rs371, under the daily fuel-price review mechanism. The new rates take effect from today, Wednesday 11 November. Petrol is now almost Rs40 a litre cheaper than its recent peak, in a second relief for motorists in under a week. (Source: ARY News / OGRA notification)

New auto policy announced: cheaper cars, EV push, $6bn export target

The federal government has unveiled a new auto policy aimed at cheaper cars and more electric vehicles. Announced by Information Minister Attaullah Tarar and Adviser Haroon Akhtar Khan, the policy rests on six pillars — affordable vehicles, exports, EVs, localisation, safety and transparency — with a target of $6 billion in auto exports over five years and advance payments capped at 20% of a vehicle's price. EV financing limits are proposed to rise to Rs10 million. The measures still need federal cabinet and IMF approval before they take effect. (Source: Islamabad Post)

PHC removes petitions against KP emergency from cause list

The Peshawar High Court has removed all petitions challenging the emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa from its cause list. The emergency itself was imposed on October 11. (Source: The Express Tribune)

Pakistani astronaut to fly to China's space station on Shenzhou-24

A Pakistani astronaut will join China's Shenzhou-24 mission as a payload specialist, the China Manned Space Agency announced on Saturday — the first foreign astronaut ever to visit the Tiangong space station. After a short stay in orbit, the astronaut will return to Earth aboard Shenzhou-23. The two candidates, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali and Khurram Daud, trained in Beijing; the final flyer and launch date have not been announced yet. (Source: Reuters)
