New auto policy announced: cheaper cars, EV push, $6bn export target

The federal government has unveiled a new auto policy aimed at cheaper cars and more electric vehicles. Announced by Information Minister Attaullah Tarar and Adviser Haroon Akhtar Khan, the policy rests on six pillars — affordable vehicles, exports, EVs, localisation, safety and transparency — with a target of $6 billion in auto exports over five years and advance payments capped at 20% of a vehicle's price. EV financing limits are proposed to rise to Rs10 million. The measures still need federal cabinet and IMF approval before they take effect. (Source: Islamabad Post)
